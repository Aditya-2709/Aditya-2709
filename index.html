FixQue

FixQue is an integrated hospital and clinic platform for appointment booking, patient management, medical records, prescriptions and billing. It connects patients, hospitals, doctors and medical stores in one system, and gives each of them a dedicated portal with role-specific features.

PatientRegisters or logs in, lands on a personal dashboard, discovers hospitals and clinics, reads their details, checks open time slots and books an appointment. The booking goes straight to the provider, and the patient can then pay.
Hospital or clinicRegisters on a dedicated page and manages everything from a hospital dashboard. All upcoming, completed and cancelled appointments are listed, and each one can be confirmed, cancelled or rescheduled.
DoctorSees the patients of their hospital, including name, age and earlier health records. After a consultation the doctor adds a new record with a prescription, generates a consultation bill for the hospital, and sends the prescription directly to a chosen medical store.
Medical storeReceives only the medicines prescribed for that patient, never their full medical history. The store then creates its own bill containing just those medicines.

Consultation charges and pharmacy charges are billed separately, so each can be handled on its own. Patients see their bills and pay by UPI or card. FixQue digitises the whole patient journey, from finding a provider to booking, consultation, prescription, pharmacy billing and payment.

Algothix OS

Algothix OS (ALX) is a lightweight Linux distribution based on Debian and made for programmers. It is terminal-first and multi-user, and it is tuned to run smoothly on old laptops with 2 to 4 GB of RAM and a spinning hard drive.

Terminal-firstThe command line is the main way to work, so the system stays fast and stays out of the way.
Multi-userSeveral people can have their own accounts and workspaces on the same machine.
Built for old hardwareRuns well on laptops with 2 to 4 GB of RAM and a hard drive, giving older machines a second life.
Connected workspaceSeveral ALX machines can work together and behave like a single workspace.
